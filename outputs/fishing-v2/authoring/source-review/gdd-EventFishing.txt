5: A=1. Overview
6: A=Fishing Event is a weather-dependent activity. It becomes available when the active weather is High Tide / Flood or Heavy Rain. Players go outdoors, face valid water, cast for free, then decide whether to buy the caught result.
7: A=Goal | B=- Give players a clear reason to react to Heavy Rain and Flood / High Tide.
- Keep fishing location-dependent and exposed outside the base.
- Make the catch a readable purchase decision instead of an automatic reward.
- Keep the pet pool and chest counters server-configured.
8: A=Core Loop | B=Qualifying weather starts → player goes outdoors → faces water → Cast is free → catch appears in hand / temporary inventory → player selects Buy or Release → counters and inventory resolve → continue while weather remains valid.
10: A=2. Event Activation & Weather
11: A=Rule | B=Details
12: A=Activation Weather | B=`HighTide` OR `HeavyRain`. `HighTide` may use Flood / Triều Cường as player-facing copy.
13: A=Heavy Rain Handling | B=Heavy Rain may represent Rain + High Tide stacking with a display-name override. Do not create a third independent weather system just for Fishing.
14: A=Event Availability | B=Fishing is active only while a qualifying weather state is active. Weather System owns the state and wave end time.
15: A=Post-event | B=No new Cast after the wave ends. An already-started Cast and any pending catch may still resolve.
16: A=Non-qualifying Weather | B=Normal, Rainy alone, Windy, GroundShake, and other states do not enable Fishing unless added to config.
18: A=3. Fishing Eligibility
19: A=Rule | B=Details
20: A=Must Face Water | B=Player must be looking toward a server-recognized water surface within configured distance and facing angle.
21: A=Indoor Restriction | B=Player cannot fish inside a house or other indoor-valid volume.
22: A=Outdoor Requirement | B=Player must be in an outdoor-valid state. Fishing does not grant base protection or invulnerability.
23: A=Cast Cost | B=Cast cost is 0 Money. Money is charged only after the player selects Buy.
24: A=Pending Catch Gate | B=A player with an unresolved PendingFishingCatch cannot Cast again.
25: A=Cast Timing | B=Cast animation, resolution delay, cooldown, and interruption rules are configurable; tune for short mobile sessions.
27: A=4. Cast & Catch Flow
28: A=Step | B=Details | C=Server Rule | D=Outcome
29: A=1. Find Water | B=Move outdoors and face valid water. | C=Validate outdoor state, water target, facing, and distance. | D=Cast becomes available.
30: A=2. Cast | B=Press Cast; rod / cast feedback plays. | C=Validate Active phase, no pending catch, player state, and cooldown. Charge 0 Money. | D=One server-owned CastID begins.
31: A=3. Resolve | B=Catch animation reveals a fish/pet or Chest 1/2/3. | C=Roll once from Fishing / chest config; stamp CatchID, result, rarity, and price. | D=One PendingFishingCatch is created.
32: A=4. Decide | B=Player selects Buy or Release / Don't Buy. | C=Buy charges stamped price atomically; Release removes only the pending offer. | D=Bought result routes to Base or temporary inventory; Release grants nothing.
33: A=5. Continue | B=Resolve the current offer before casting again. | C=One pending catch per player; update counters once per successful Cast. | D=Cast again while weather and eligibility remain valid.
35: A=5. Catch Outcome Probability
36: A=Rule | B=Details
37: A=Outcome | B=Each successful Cast resolves into exactly one Fish / pet, Chest 1, Chest 2, or Chest 3 outcome.
38: A=Chest Counters | B=Chest 1, Chest 2, and Chest 3 each use an independent catch counter.
39: A=Rate Lookup | B=Read the current value of each counter from the configured rate table.
40: A=Fish Rate | B=Fish % = 100% - Chest 1% - Chest 2% - Chest 3%.
41: A=Pet-pool Pity | B=No pet-pool pity or guaranteed pet counter. Chest guarantees are a separate system.
42: A=Price Formula | B=For pet results, ClaimPrice = SpawnPrice × ClaimK. Stamp the result and price when the catch resolves.
44: A=6. Hand / Temporary Inventory Resolution
45: A=State | B=Details | C=Player Experience | D=Inventory Effect
46: A=Pending Catch | B=Create one server-owned PendingFishingCatch with CatchID, result ID, rarity, stamped price, source band, and counter snapshot. | C=Result appears in the player's hand with an offer card. | D=Temporary inventory is the recoverable pending record; the result is not yet owned.
47: A=Buy | B=Validate CatchID, Money, capacity, and player state; deduct the stamped price atomically. | C=Show exact Money deduction before confirmation. | D=Route to Base if a slot exists; otherwise route to Event Pet Inventory if capacity exists.
48: A=Release / Don't Buy | B=Confirm if needed, then delete the pending CatchID. | C=Clearly state that the result will be lost. | D=Charge 0; grant 0.
49: A=Base Full / Buffer Full | B=Do not charge until a valid destination exists. | C=Keep the catch pending and show the required action. | D=Never silently discard or auto-replace another pet.
50: A=Close / Disconnect | B=Persist the pending catch by CatchID and restore it on re-entry. | C=One offer card only; no second Cast until resolved. | D=No reroll, duplicate pet, or duplicate chest bundle.

67: A=9. Catch Resolution Order
68: A=Step | B=Details
69: A=1 | B=Validate qualifying weather: High Tide / Flood or Heavy Rain must be active when Cast begins.
70: A=2 | B=Validate player state: outdoors, facing valid water, no pending catch, and valid Cast timing.
71: A=3 | B=Increment all three chest counters once for the successful Cast attempt.
72: A=4 | B=Check hard guarantees in priority order: Chest 3 → Chest 2 → Chest 1.
73: A=5 | B=If a guarantee exists, award the highest guaranteed Chest and apply its reset rule.
74: A=6 | B=Otherwise read Chest rates from the current counters and calculate Fish as the remainder.
75: A=7 | B=Roll one result and stamp CatchID, result, rarity, and purchase price.
76: A=8 | B=Persist the hand / temporary-inventory offer; resolve Buy or Release before another Cast.
