8: A=Whack-a-Mole is a timed co-op event where players in a room whack moles as they pop up from underground. Every 10 minutes, a Mole surfaces. Its HP scales with the number of active players in the room — 40 HP per player, capped at 240 HP. | H=            
9: A=Goal | B=- Introduce Limited Time Pet (3/account).
- Swapable with other events for event duration (ex: 14 days).
- Create a predictable multiplayer moment that encourages players to stay active together.
- Reward room participation instead of solo farming.
- Add a simple recurring event loop with clear short-term rewards and a longer-term exchange goal. | H=
