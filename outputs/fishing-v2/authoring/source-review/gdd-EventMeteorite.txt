12: A=2. Event Flow
13: A=Rule Name | B=Rules
14: A=Spawn | B=Every900 seconds (15 minutes). Use server room timer; no extra spawn on login. One Meteor per scheduled spawn. | F=3.0
15: A=Meteor HP | B=HP =min(40×room occupants at spawn,240), minimum40. Snapshot once; do not change HP on join/leave. Reuse existing event entity.
16: A=Damage | B=4 HP per accepted player/clone hit. Count only server-accepted damage. Misses, dead targets and repeated hit IDs give no counter progress.
17: A=Currency | B=Stars / Sao. Event-only wallet keyed by EventID. Shard stack size does not multiply Stars. Old shard balance is not converted.
18: A=Owner | B=Player and owned clone hits use one owner counter and wallet. Clone has no separate cap or wallet. Idle clones give no hit progress.
19: A=Counter start | B=At each new Meteor, set owner HitCount=0 and pick Threshold uniformly from integers4,5,6,7,8,9. Unfinished progress expires with that Meteor.
20: A=Counter payout | B=Each accepted owner hit adds1. At HitCount≥Threshold: emit one payout result, grant10 Stars subject to cap, reset HitCount=0 and pick a new4–9 Threshold. Repeat on the same Meteor.
21: A=One-hit contributor | B=A fresh single hit gives no threshold reward. Progress and payouts use the player's own accepted hits, not total room damage.
22: A=Break / other drops | B=No extra Star reward on break and no separate random Star roll. Existing non-currency drops stay unchanged. Meteor Stars are granted only through counter payouts.
23: A=Daily cap | B=100 earned Stars/account/day, shared with activities. Grant=min(10,max(0,100−EarnedToday)). Spending does not reopen the cap. No wallet-balance cap.
24: A=After cap | B=Keep gameplay and existing non-Star drops. Payout counter may continue; credited Stars remain0 until daily reset. Do not bank missed Stars.
25: A=Reward retry | B=Use EventID + MeteorInstanceID + OwnerID + PayoutIndex as result key. Serialize counter update, completion key and wallet grant; retries do not pay twice.
26: A=Counter reconnect | B=Save current MeteorInstanceID, HitCount, Threshold and PayoutIndex. Reconnect to the same instance resumes saved state; another Meteor starts a fresh counter.
27: A=Daily reset | B=UTC midnight: DayKey=floor(ServerUnixSec/86400). Reset EarnedToday and activity counters/claims. Keep wallet and shop purchase counts. Meteor counter follows MeteorInstanceID, not daily reset.
28: A=Duration | B=Earning14 days; shop stays open3 more days. EndUnix=StartUnix+14×86400. ShopCloseUnix=EndUnix+3×86400. Start at UTC reset boundary. | F= 
29: A=Event end | B=EndUnix stops earning. ShopCloseUnix stops purchases and expires remaining Stars. Use Event Frame transaction/retry rules.
