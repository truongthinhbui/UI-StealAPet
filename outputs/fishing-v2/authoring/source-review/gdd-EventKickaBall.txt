7: A=1. Overview
8: A=Lucky Kick is a 21-day event built on a grab-and-kick loop. Balls spawn in the room on a 10-minute cycle. Players race to grab one and carry it home — carry is protected, no one can steal from you in transit. At home, a mini-game lets you kick the ball for Gems and other rewards. Each ball holds a finite Gems pool; once the pool empties, the ball stops earning gems. Pool size is hidden from the player.

Headline prize: [Event Exclusive Pet], event-exclusive. 1 per account. | H=

- Create a predictable multiplayer moment every 10 minutes that rewards players staying in an active room.

- Reward consistent daily play through shop price tiers unlocking over time.
