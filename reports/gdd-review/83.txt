1: A=UNLOCKING RULE | C=Draft — 3.0.0. Paid force unlock.
2: C=Stealing stays risky. Repeated paid breaks get a short block.
4: A=1. Overview
5: A=Summary | B=Keep stealing. Change only paid force-unlock price and repeat cooldown.
6: A=Goal | B=Give high-RB players time to recover after a paid break. Big RB gaps cost more.
7: A=Currency | B=Pet Coin only. No key, diamond bypass, or money cost.
8: A=Core loop | B=Base locked → see price → pay Pet Coin → base opens → defender cooldown starts.
9: A=Out of scope | B=Natural opening, steal rules, carrying, laser wall, storage, Favorite Pet, and normal relock stay the same.

13: A=Static price | B=Read one number from the table below. That is the final Pet Coin price.

15: A=RB gap | B=Use the RB difference band: <3, 4–6, 7–9, or 10+.
16: A=Direction | B=Same price whether the attacker has higher or lower RB.
17: A=No valid target | B=If the attacker cannot steal any pet: block paid unlock and charge nothing. This is for lower level RB player who cannot steal higher rarity
18: A=Confirm | B=Show the final Pet Coin price. Check target and cooldown again when the player confirms.

42: A=Cooldown Rules | B=Details
43: A=Start | B=Starts after a paid force unlock opens a locked base.
44: A=Cooldown owner | B=Defender account only.
45: A=Shared block | B=Everyone must wait to paid unlock this defender. Changing attacker or account does not help.
46: A=Does not block | B=Natural entry or stealing from an already-open base.
47: A=Relock | B=Normal relock rules still apply. Relocking does not reset cooldown.
48: A=No charge | B=Do not charge or start cooldown if blocked, failed, no target, or base is open.
49: A=Keep timer | B=Keep the defender timer through reconnect and server hop. It keeps counting offline.
50: A=RB change | B=Keep an active timer. Read defender RB only when the next paid unlock starts a new timer.
51: A=Known tradeoff | B=Friends can pay once to start protection. Accept this small issue; they cannot refresh the timer and the base is still exposed.

60: A=5. Pre-ship Checks
61: A=Price table | B=Use only the four listed RB-gap bands. Keep this GDD and UnlockCost the same.
62: A=No modifiers | B=Use the table number directly. No multiplier, extra price, or rounding.
63: A=Group opening | B=Confirm whether one paid opening also helps other attackers.
64: A=Atomic payment | B=At payment, check target, price, base state, and defender cooldown again. Charge only if the opening succeeds.
65: A=Tracking | B=Log attacker RB, defender RB, rarity, gap band, price, cooldown start/end, repeat paid breaks, and theft.
66: A=Success read | B=Check repeat paid breaks, fast repeat losses, exits by defender RB, valid raid targets, and Pet Coin spend.
