7: A=1. System Overview
8: A=A shared boss spawns on the open map (not at the player's own base) at a fixed interval. Players deal melee damage directly; their equipped combat pet auto-attacks alongside them. Damage converts to Dust. 
9: A=Boss reward scales with the combined Rebirth of everyone fighting it. Dust is earned continuously as damage lands
