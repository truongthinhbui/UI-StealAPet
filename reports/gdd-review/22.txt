13: A=2. Core Mechanics & Rules
14: A=Rule Name | B=Rules
15: A=Linear Tracking | B=Missions are sequential. The player only ever sees and works on ONE mission at a time.
16: A=Condition Types | B=1. Action-based: Player must trigger an event (e.g., "Buy a Pet", "Fuse a Pet").
2. State-based: Player must reach a threshold (e.g., "Have 10,000 Cash", "Unlock Base Level 2").
17: A=Retroactive Completion | B=If a player reaches a State-based mission and already meets the requirements (e.g., Mission is "Have 6 Pets" and they already have 6), the mission instantly switches to the "Claim" state.
18: A=Action Tracking | B=Action-based missions only start listening for events after the mission becomes active. (e.g., If they buy a pet on Step 1, it does not count for the "Buy a Pet" requirement on Step 4).
19: A=Claim Logic | B=Rewards are NOT auto-granted. The player MUST click "Claim" to receive the items. This forces interaction and provides the dopamine hit.
20: A=Subsidized Economy | B=Rewards are heavily weighted towards Cash and specific Pet IDs to mathematically guarantee the player can afford their early Rebirths without relying on RNG.
22: A=3. UI / UX Rules
23: A=Component | B=Details
24: A=HUD Widget | B=Small UI box on the right of the screen. Shows: [Mission Title], [Progress: 0/1], and [Reward Icon + Value].
25: A=No Interruptions | B=No pop-ups. The mission UI never hijacks the camera or forces the player to stop moving.
