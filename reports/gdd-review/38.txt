75: A=Output grade | B=SSS
76: A=Buy | B=Pet Coins. 
77: A=Use | B=Boss Shard
78: A=Why it exists | B=It is the only source of Prismatic / Vortex / Sequin charms (§5.1). Rare-weather pets become charm material — a real decision: keep the pet, fuse it, or break it.
79: A=Exploit note | B=Cheap tagged Common → extract → charm a top pet. Self-limited: apply chance on Apex/Divine = base × 0.2 (≈10% for SSS-grade base 50%), plus the money UseCost at target level, plus the deleted pet. Verify vs. expected fusion cost in the balance pass anyway.
