SHEET Fusion Design
1: A=The Fusion System allows players to combine any two pets to create a new pet.
2: A=The resulting pet’s rarity and mutation are determined by probabilistic formulas that depend on the rarity difference between the two pets used as materials.
3: A=This system encourages experimentation, pet collection, and progression through controlled randomness.
5: A=1. Fusion Process
6: A=Step | C=Description
7: A=1. Select Base Pet (Pet 1) | C=Interact with one pet, select Fusion button, this pet will be displayed as the primary pet
8: A=2. Select Material Pet (Pet 2) | C=select a secondary pet
9: A=3. Confirm Fusion | C=Display fusion cost, duration, and rate breakdown.
-> click Fusion.
10: A=4. Fusion Cost | C=Cost = MAX(Cost(Pet1), Cost(Pet2)) × 2
11: A=5. Hatch Duration | C=Hatch Duration = MAX(Rarity1, Rarity2) × 5 sec
12: A=6. Start Fusion | C=Pet 1 transforms into an Egg, Pet 2 disappears.
13: A=7. Egg Phase | C=Egg cannot be stolen, sold, or interacted with until hatch time ends.
14: A=8. Hatch Interaction | C=After countdown, player taps the egg to reveal result UI.
15: A=9. Select Pet Result | C=Display a UI with 3 pets, allowing the player to select 1 of the 3: Pet1, Pet2, or the fusion result.
If players select pet1 or pet2 instead of the result, they need to pay money (pet coin = rarity * 10)
17: A=2. Result Logic
18: A=Result Type | C=Description | F=Formula / Condition
19: A=High Result | C=Result = Pet with higher rarity among the two pets used. | F=Rate_High = (1 - Rate_Secret)/2 - log(diff+1,6)*0.5
20: A=Low Result | C=Result = Pet with lower rarity among the two pets used. | F=Rate_Low = (1 - Rate_Secret)/2 + log(diff+1,6)*0.5
21: A=Secret Result | C=Result = random pet from Secret Pool (based on lower rarity). | F=Rate_Secret = 0.01+(RARITY_COUNT-1-diff)^2*0.02
22: F=RARITY_COUNT = 5
