5: A=1. Overview
6: A=Windnest Tree is a short Windy-weather acquisition event. A giant tree grows in the shared map for approximately five minutes. Players leave their exposed base, choose a Low, Mid, or High branch, quickly collect private priced sealed-egg offers into a Wind Basket, purchase selected eggs in one step, then hatch them at home.
7: A=The fun target is a visible shared ascent with frequent upward reward beats, a memorable Crown Nest summit, and an occasional Golden Tree. Traversal is shared, but offers, baskets, and purchases are private. There is no contribution system, leaderboard, top-contributor bonus, FCFS egg, or winner-take-all reward.
